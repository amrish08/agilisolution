Service Delivery Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Nature of services

Agile Solution provides computer and laptop repair and technical support services. Booking a service does not ordinarily involve shipping a physical product.

How service is fulfilled

After a booking is received, our team contacts the customer to confirm the appointment, service location, device issue, and next steps. Work begins only after arrangements are confirmed.

Service timing

The selected appointment date and time are preferences, not guaranteed delivery deadlines. Actual repair completion time depends on diagnosis, parts availability, issue complexity, and customer approval. We will communicate any material delay.

Parts or device movement

If a service requires replacement parts or the movement of a device, the arrangement, applicable charges, and expected timing will be discussed with the customer before proceeding.
