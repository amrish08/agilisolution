Effective date: 20 November 2026

Booking advance

Agile Solution collects a ₹500 advance for a service booking.

Full refund before work begins

If you cancel before any service work begins, you are entitled to a full refund of the ₹500 booking advance.

How to cancel

Request cancellation by emailing amrishselvam08@gmail.com or calling +91 88259 85850. Please include your booking reference, name, contact number, and payment reference if available. We will confirm whether work has begun.

After work begins

If service work has already begun, the automatic full-refund rule for the booking advance no longer applies. Any refund or charges will depend on work performed, agreed terms, and applicable consumer law. We will explain the position before resolving the request.

Refund method and timing

Approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method where supported. Actual credit timing depends on the bank and payment provider. Contact us if an approved refund has not appeared within 7–10 business days after initiation.

Payment issues

For duplicate payments or unsuccessful payments where money was debited, contact us with the transaction reference so we can investigate and coordinate with the payment provider.
